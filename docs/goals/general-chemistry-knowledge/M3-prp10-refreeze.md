# M3 — PRP-10 Refreeze

## Objective

Freeze a new ten-paper lockbox after M2 development so the selected settings
and knowledge configuration is evaluated on untouched evidence.

## Eligibility and freeze

Do not reuse any paper whose outcome informed registry rules, knowledge packs,
prompts, validators, graders, or architecture decisions. Select one eligible
paper for each active PRP-10 slot: mechanism/TS/IRC, transition-metal
spin/basis/ECP, excited state, conformer/noncovalent ensemble,
thermochemistry/standard state, QM/MM or multilevel, open shell, constrained
scan, explicit cluster, and multilevel workflow.

Before opening outcomes, independently verify full text, SI, access/license,
critical methods, and an official exact single-frame XYZ in angstrom. Bind
archive member, exact bytes, atom order, identity approval, charge,
multiplicity, and source provenance. A missing critical fact makes the paper
ineligible; do not repair coordinates, infer consequential settings, contact
authors, or add unreported sensitivity work.

Freeze paper IDs and source hashes, K0 and selected M2D configuration, registry
and knowledge-pack versions, prompts, model/thinking mode, tools, budgets,
order, project loaders, CLI schema, compiler, safe-preview validators, three
review packets, graders, and analysis code. An external or independently owned
coverage manifest must define required calculations before the planner runs.

## Evaluation

Run the frozen single-agent K0 reference and selected configuration under the
preregistered paired protocol. For every paper require evidence-addressed
claims, molecular/electronic-state specifications, loader-valid project YAML,
canonical command DAG, artifact bindings, safe previews for expressible nodes,
validation/analysis/failure plans, and three fresh read-only reviews. Keep
turn, tool, plan, and scientific outcomes separate.

## Exit gate

Report every paper and failure, including cost and latency. The engineering
acceptance target may remain `10/10 paper_complete_pass@1` with zero safety
violations, but it is not a general SOTA claim. Once outcomes are opened, this
lockbox becomes development data; no prompt, rule, validator, or pack may be
tuned and then reevaluated as if still held out. Run the preregistered M3 gate
once and do not rerun for tuning.
