# Frontier P6 replication and training no-go decision

## Current decision

Do not publish, train, release, invoke a model, run a chemistry engine, or
claim SOTA from this package. This is an evidence and authority decision, not a
judgment that future work cannot succeed.

## Clean-replication requirements

A future independent replication claim needs all of the following:

1. A frozen candidate that hashes every receipt, source/fixture/scorer revision,
   report, claim register, citation snapshot, and environment specification.
2. An independently controlled clean pinned environment that reconstructs the
   declared fixture/report/score outputs; its receipt must retain environment
   identity, command, output hashes, comparison tolerance, and observed result.
3. For every numerical or comparative statement: raw native or archived
   artifact, units/conventions, deterministic validator output, source support,
   and correction-status limitation where applicable.
4. If environment construction would install dependencies, separate explicit
   approval before that action. A reconstruction in an already available
   independent environment remains a separate evidence task, not an implied
   release approval.

## Controlled-comparison requirements

A scientific or SOTA comparison additionally needs the complete frozen P5
study: all eight configurations; at least three paired repetitions per
externally held-out case; a fixed provider/model/tool/prompt/budget surface;
deterministic-first grading; retained raw outcomes/failures/exhaustions; paired
95% intervals; named comparators; and independent replication. P3 public cases
and source-controlled grader seeds remain development material, never held-out
evidence.

## Training eligibility checklist

| Requirement | Current state | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visible verified action/outcome traces | No observed model/engine traces. | Not eligible. |
| Deterministic labels tied to evidence | P3 fixture labels are development controls only. | Not eligible. |
| External held-out anti-hacking boundary | No holder or commitment exists. | Not eligible. |
| Scientific-result trace with units/diagnostics | No result artifact or recomputation exists. | Not eligible. |
| Explicit data, model, dependency, compute, and training authority | Not granted. | Not eligible. |

Eligible training records: **0**. Hidden reasoning, raw provider transcripts,
credentials, and grader-only seeds are categorically excluded from any future
training corpus.

## Material decision required before a live P5/P6 path

The user would need to authorize, separately and explicitly: a validated
provider capability/configuration path within an existing-quota ceiling; an
independent held-out evaluator/custody model; any real chemistry-engine or
scheduler action; and any environment/dependency, training, compute,
publication, commit, or push action. Those permissions do not imply one
another.
