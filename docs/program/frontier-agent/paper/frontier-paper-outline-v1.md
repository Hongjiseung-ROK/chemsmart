## Evidence-derived methods outline

1. **Program scope and authority.** Describe the CLI-first boundary, original
   checkout preservation, and zero-use restrictions from P0.
2. **Runtime evidence contracts.** Describe P2 only as additive typed payload,
   replay, approval-record, and CLI-characterization infrastructure. Do not
   describe it as executor-side dispatch enforcement.
3. **Fixture-only fault harness.** Describe the frozen P3 zero-call reference,
   public cases, grader-only seed separation, and deterministic correct-block
   criterion. State that no model outcome or physical calculation was observed.
4. **Evidence review.** Describe P4 as a hash-pinned, read-only role process
   with explicit stop conditions. Do not represent it as five commissioned
   independent human expert reviews or a critic calibration result.
5. **Evaluation protocol.** Describe P5 as an unexecuted eight-configuration,
   three-repetition-per-future-held-out-case preregistration. State that its
   live envelope is unset and zero-call.
6. **Literature method.** Use P1 only for its named narrow propositions and
   retain the primary-passage and correction-status limitations.
